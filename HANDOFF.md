## Subject data format (`window.SUBJECT_DATA[id]`)
- `units`: `[{id, name, title, lectures, w?}]`. `w` is the unit weight for MCQ mocks.
- `notes`: `[{id, unit, lec, title, sum, html}]`
- `bank` (MCQ): `[topicId, question, correct, [3 wrong], explanation]`. Options are shuffled at runtime; the id is a hash of the question text.
  A question containing `\n` renders the text after the first newline as a code block.
- `short`: `[topic, q, [[point, marks, "kw|kw"]]]`. Rubric keywords are regexes used to pre-tick points.
- `longq`: same shape as `short`, but 10 marks.
- `long` (cases): `{id, title, topics, text, parts: [[q, marks, points]]}`
- Also: `casesets`, `cases`, `cards [topic, front, back]`, `revision {unitId: html}`, `videos [ytId, topic, title]`, `guide` (html).

## Exam formats
- **PBM** (24 Oct 2026, 9:30–12:30): official end-term format, `paper.format = "4sec"`, 100 marks, 3 hours.
  - Section A: 10 × 2 or 20 × 1 MCQs = 20 marks (randomly picked per paper).
  - Section B: attempt any 5 of 6 short answers, 6 marks each, max 100 words. The 5-mark rubrics are scaled to 6 at load.
  - Section C: attempt any 3 of 4 long answers, 10 marks each, max 500 words.
  - Section D: one compulsory case study, 2 questions × 10 marks (2 parts of a `long` case, scaled to 10).
  - Code: `buildExam` / `vPaper` / `paperPrint` in `app.js`. Paper state is saved in localStorage with `v: 2`; old-format papers are discarded.
- **Programming** (31 Oct 2026, 9:30–11:30, computer-based): `paper.type = "mcq"`, 100 MCQs in 120 minutes, weighted by unit (25/20/30/25).

## Voice answers (v5)
- Every answer `textarea` in `#view` gets a 🎙️ **Speak** button, attached automatically by a MutationObserver (`attachMic`).
- Web Speech API (`en-IN`) types the speech into the box, firing an `input` event so the normal save handlers run.
- At the same time, `MediaRecorder` records the audio and stores it in IndexedDB (`br_voice`, keyed by the answer box key). It plays back under the box.
- The transcript is appended to that topic's **My notes** (`S.mynotes[topic]`), or to "Voice notes" when there's no topic, and synced to Firebase.
- Firefox has no dictation, so it records audio only. iOS Safari dictation may stop between phrases; recognition auto-restarts.
